Review rubric

Normal (normal_core): based only on the current six-camera frame, conditions are clear and routine, with no visible factor requiring extra defensive driving. Outside the normal core (not_normal_core): visible low visibility, perception difficulties from night or rain, key occlusions, obstacles, construction, complex traffic, or road users clearly too close to or near the ego vehicle's path require extra defensive action such as slowing down, yielding or increasing the safety margin.
