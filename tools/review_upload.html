Labelling rubric (the English text frozen during data preparation)

Judge only what is visible in the current six-camera frame. Normal means ordinary, clear conditions requiring no extra defensive driving. Outside normal core means extra conservative driving is clearly needed or static difficulty factors are present. Choose uncertain when the images do not support a stable judgment. Apply the rubric below to night, low visibility, construction and occlusions. Do not infer temporal events such as hard braking or sudden lane changes from a single frame.
