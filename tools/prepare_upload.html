Create a small uploadable sample from a large archive

Files are read only in your browser. This page makes no network requests and uploads nothing. Open it in desktop Chrome or Edge.

  1. Select your downloaded v1.0-mini.tgz, .tar.gz or .tar.
  2. Click "Create parts" and keep the page open. The archive is read sequentially twice; this may take a few minutes.
  3. Download all ZIP parts, then upload them to the chat. The original archive remains unchanged.

Keeps only five required metadata tables and the six camera images for each selected frame. Images are not cropped and labels are not created. The default two frames support an end-to-end trial, not a promise of formal comparison results. Each part is at most 24 MiB. The original data terms still apply.
